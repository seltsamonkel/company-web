Keep Notifying Me: foreground service demonstration

The optional Prefer phone speaker feature uses a mediaPlayback foreground service to play a brief reminder sound. This recording shows the user enabling the feature, triggering a test reminder, and checking that playback stops automatically. A second test shows cancellation.

Open or download the MP4 recording · 1 minute 37 seconds · no audio or narration

Steps demonstrated

  1. Open Setup and enable Precise timing.
  2. Enable Prefer phone speaker and review its disclosure.
  3. Tap Test speaker reminder to start playback; observe automatic stopping.
  4. Open troubleshooting and diagnostics to inspect playback status.
  5. Run a second test and cancel playback.
